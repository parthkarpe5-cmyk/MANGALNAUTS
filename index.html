MANGALNAUTS — Planetary Multi-Site Operations & GNN Traversability

Designed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026: Interplanetary Survival Guide (Martian Map). Pulls together data from NASA Mars missions into an operational cockpit for human Marswalk and rover mission routing.

VERIFIED SCIENTIFIC DATA CITATIONS

  • NASA Mars Science Laboratory (MSL): Curiosity REMS live daily in-situ atmospheric telemetry (4,745 sols in Gale Crater) & 33.28 km PDS Mount Sharp traverse.
  • NASA Mars 2020: Perseverance MEDA live in-situ weather telemetry & 42.71 km PDS Western Delta traverse.
  • NASA Odyssey GRS: Global elemental concentration maps (Water-Equivalent Hydrogen, Fe, Si, K, Th, Cl) from Boynton et al. 2007 (PDS Geosciences Node).
  • Topography: USGS HRSC-MOLA 200m Blended DEM (Fergason et al. 2018) & MGS MOLA MEGDR elevation data.
  • Orbital Spectroscopy & Thermal: ASU THEMIS Day/Night IR & Thermal Inertia (Edwards et al.) + CRISM hyperspectral phyllosilicate clays and carbonates (Murchie et al.).
  • 3D spacecraft models · NASA Science, JPL, Ames Research Center, and Brian E. Kumanchik. Models are enlarged for visibility.
  • Space Weather: NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (SWPC) live 1-minute GOES X-ray solar flare flux & MSL RAD cosmic ray dosimetry.
  • AI / Navigation Engine: Vectorized 2-Layer Graph Convolutional Network (GCN) running spatial topological message passing for multi-task traversability and biosignature ranking.
